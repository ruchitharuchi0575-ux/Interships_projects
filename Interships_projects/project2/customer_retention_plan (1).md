
# Customer Retention Plan

## Objective
Improve customer retention using RFM and cohort analysis.

## Strategies

1. Loyal Customers:
Offer loyalty rewards and exclusive discounts.

2. At-Risk Customers:
Use personalised offers to encourage repeat purchases.

3. Big Spenders:
Provide special benefits and premium offers.

4. Recent Customers:
Encourage second purchases through welcome offers.

## Recommendation
Prioritise customers who have not purchased recently.
Use personalised marketing to encourage repeat purchases.

## Conclusion
RFM and cohort analysis help businesses understand
customer behaviour and improve customer retention.
