# Where Is My Train Executive KPI Tree

## 1. User & Rider Volume (Adoption & Activity)
├── Total Daily Active Users (DAU) and Monthly Active Users (MAU)
├── New App Installs & Organic Growth Rate (%)
├── Session Frequency (Average checks per user per day)
└── Route Search Volume (Total train status queries per day)

## 2. Peak Share & Usage Concentration (Traffic Patterns)
├── Peak Commute Share (%) 
│   ├── Morning Peak Hour Queries (7:00 AM – 11:00 AM)
│   └── Evening Peak Hour Queries (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM)
├── Festive / Holiday Season Surge Factor (% increase during Diwali, Pongal, etc.)
└── High-Demand Corridor Concentration (Top 10 most queried railway zones or routes)

## 3. Revenue Proxies & Monetization (Value & Engagement)
├── In-App Advertisement Monetization Proxies
│   ├── Ad Impressions per Session
│   └── Average Click-Through Rate (CTR) on Banner or Interstitial Ads
├── Partner Ecosystem & Ticketing Referral Proxies
│   ├── Outbound Clicks to Partner Ticketing/Booking Platforms (IRCTC partners)
│   └── Booking Conversion Rate (%) from App Searches
└── Feature Engagement Proxies
    ├── Offline Network Cell-Tower Tracking Usage (%)
    └── PNR Status Check Frequency per User
